## 3. Design, learn, validate and animate

Read [the Figma and Motion Study workflow](references/motion-study-figma.md). Open or create the actual Figma file through the Figma skills. Save editable start/end designs and intermediate states needed for reveals, depth turns or content changes. Generate stable artwork once and retain independent layers for elements that move separately. Save the Figma file URL, page name, node links and exported layer hashes in the production record and review page so the user can find and edit the designs.

Copycat then measures source movement at native cadence. Separate camera, page, object and cursor movement. Record position, scale, rotation/depth, clipping, layer order, event onset, holds and cuts. Future frames are evidence for trajectories; endpoints alone do not recover the intervening motion. Validate the measurements with the available Motion Study skill and its later audit corrections before animating in Codex.

For text, title assembly, word offsets and collage motion, read [the Present Flow integration](references/present-flow.md). Match the shot to an approved motion family, export its exact control/expression contract with `scripts/present_flow.py`, and retain its native timing semantics before sampling at 12 fps. The bundled approved AEP is the full-fidelity authority for that kit mechanism; the source video remains the authority for other reference sequences. Keep broken/incomplete variants out of automatic production selection. Package integrity and extracted keys do not prove a faithful Codex port: validate against native evaluated poses or validated samples before claiming accuracy.

Render the fixed assets on source-indexed poses at 12 fps. A 3.0-second sequence requires 36 output frames with exact source times. Preserve sharp instantaneous motion, identity, material and intentional depth of field. Do not substitute a generic pan/zoom, crossfade or universal easing curve for the measured motion. A 2D sprite does not reproduce a 3D turn; use appropriate additional views/geometry or disclose the unresolved approximation.

For the user's requested illusion of 3D object rotation, generate a separate transparent multi-view image sequence per object, then play its selected poses in that object's animation layer along the source path. Lock identity, pivot, physical scale, camera and light; change perspective and occlusion in the images. Do not simulate depth by squeezing the same front view. Follow the [object-turn procedure](references/motion-study-figma.md#object-turn-sequences).

If the user explicitly selects the original full-frame image-generation route, read [the legacy frame workflow](references/frame-workflow.md). Its whole-frame generation requirements apply only to that route. Do not silently alternate methods between frames.

Test a full moving pilot of at least three seconds before scaling the same method. Inspect and correct actual drift. A failed pilot does not authorize an approximate full batch. Keep other sources inventoried and pending; continue independent source/design work. Retry with a specific correction to the demonstrated cause; if it recurs without a credible remedy, report the missing capability and show the failed diagnostic clearly.
