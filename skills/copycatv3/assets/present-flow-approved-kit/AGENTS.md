# Project guidance

Read README.md and docs/KNOWN-ISSUES.md first. The collected AE project and approved catalog supersede legacy browser-only assumptions. SH20 arc rigs are excluded as reference material.

Do not impose one Progress driver on all families. Preserve keyframed, expression-driven and constant control contracts. Keep home/layout/motion distinct; document actual timing units and whether cadence is score-relative or real-time. Do not silently fix or alter approved art direction.

Archived JSX scripts are not guaranteed to reproduce the collected snapshots. Do not run them against the approved project without checking preconditions and working on a duplicate. Never treat local font availability or linked-file existence as proof of transferable assets.

Do not commit secrets or publish the repository without authorization. Fonts and third-party imagery retain their existing rights.
