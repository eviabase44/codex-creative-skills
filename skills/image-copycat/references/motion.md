# Motion branch

Use Copycat's source-frame workflow and the motion study's effective audit. Inspect the chosen source in motion, extract every decoded selected frame with timestamps, and retain the source-to-output map. Native 24/25 fps is source-specific. A 12 fps delivery is a separately requested derivative sampled by time, never a speed change or an assumed universal requirement.

For each shot record source range, narrative purpose, immutable graphic transforms, camera versus object motion, size/angle, light direction, lens character where observable, replacement content and start/end state. Label inferred focal length as an inference. A 2D graphic has no photographic camera; do not add a fake camera move. Source fidelity takes precedence over generic suggestions to add dynamism.

Camera Language: keep the source's framing and one motivated camera move where present; preserve axis, eyeline, light direction and prop state. Eye Candy: name the source's actual technique and explain the join's story purpose; match outgoing/incoming position, scale, direction, masks and overlap. Do not add a whip, morph or transition absent from the reference. Acting Director: new performance needs a clear stimulus, an observable response and a stable eyeline; match expression/gesture scale to framing and retain state across cuts. Frame-accurate performance is a result to validate, not a prompting guarantee.

For new photographic content, carry Image Bible light, materials and natural performance into the actual prompt. Keep source graphic layers graphic. Verify current engine controls before promising timing, reference conditioning or custom weights. No model is selected by this skill.

Render a short moving proof covering the riskiest substitution, including deformation, occlusion or transitions. Compare internal movement as well as exterior boxes. If the tool only produces plausible still variants, that is not adequate evidence of a moving replacement. Stop scaling a method whose proof fails.

Keep source sound only if appropriate to the new context. Strip unrelated spoken claims for adapted posts; preserve or replace music/FX according to the brief and available assets. Do not claim silent output is a finished spoken film. Preserve full audio tail when retained.

Before `ready_for_review`, verify every output frame is mapped, source timestamps/cadence and authorized duration/aspect are respected, decoding succeeds, no black gaps or truncated ending remain, and retained/replaced audio matches the brief. Inspect aligned playback, typography, logo, texture, continuity and readability separately. Retain proof footage, frame map and concrete inspection notes with hashes. A still test does not validate this branch.
