# Location direction

Use this guide to select the location image in the story's character-and-location pair, then follow the [sub-skill's workflow](../SKILL.md#copy-the-location-then-change-five-elements). Copy its spatial and photographic construction, then remix exactly five visible location elements alongside the character remix from the parent skill. The group descriptions below are selection cues; the actual inspected image takes precedence over typical palettes or lighting recipes. These are visual interpretations of the collected references, not claims about the real architecture, location or authorship of a pin. Check the current manifest and review decisions before selecting an anchor.

## L01 — Layered loft interiors

**Character:** Spacious industrial bones, made personal through accumulated objects. High ceilings, tall multi-pane windows, exposed concrete or brick, a mezzanine or open plan where useful. Contrast the shell with softened leather, linen, vintage rugs, books, plants and a few bold artworks.

**Light and palette:** Window light crossing the floor, neutral concrete with warm timber, olive upholstery, faded blue and restrained rust or orange. Keep useful color; the brighter eclectic references are a separate optional accent branch, not a requirement for maximalism.

**Composition:** Let the window, seating and one meaningful foreground object establish depth. Keep verticals and furniture scale credible. Favor a human viewpoint over extreme real-estate ultrawide stretching. The room should support the requested action, rather than become an empty architecture showcase.

**Inspected candidates:** L01-021, L01-024, L01-039, L01-054, L01-058 and L01-081. Their eligibility still depends on live review state.

## L02 — Collected, practical kitchens

**Character:** An everyday kitchen with functional surfaces and a visible personality. The seed combines stainless appliances, a stove, tile, open shelves, exposed ducts, a simple stool and small framed objects. Related references add timber counters, colored cabinet fronts, everyday ceramics and books.

**Light and palette:** Side daylight with plausible falloff; warm wood against stainless, cream tile and muted green. A red kettle, colored pendant or graphic print can supply one stronger accent. Preserve the different reflectance of metal, ceramic, glass and matte paint.

**Composition:** Keep the useful working area legible and leave room for believable cooking or conversation. A few occupied shelves and a towel feel lived in; indiscriminate clutter does not. Product diagrams, unrelated living rooms and storefronts from the recommendation feed are flagged or need separate inspection.

**Inspected candidates:** L02-001 (user seed), L02-003, L02-020, L02-043, L02-083 and L02-099.

## L03 — Urban rooftop living

**Character:** An outdoor room above the city. The seed uses low pallet-like seating, worn timber, layered rugs, relaxed cushions, books and potted plants against a distant skyline. Preserve that intimate scale instead of turning every rooftop into a hotel roof bar.

**Light and palette:** Late-afternoon side light or open shade, weathered brown, cream, olive and muted terracotta. For a requested evening setting, practical lamps, lanterns or a few strings of bulbs can create small warm pools against a cooler city. Daylight remains the core Image Bible treatment.

**Composition:** Show enough railing or parapet and skyline to establish a roof, with a close seating arrangement and one foreground plant. Preserve plausible outdoor materials, circulation and gravity. Indoor rooms, cafes and decorative details in the related feed are companion mood candidates, not evidence of rooftop architecture.

**Inspected candidates:** L03-066 (user seed), L03-022, L03-023, L03-037, L03-046, L03-068, L03-085 and L03-097.

## L04 — Neighborhood restaurants and cafes

**Character:** Sociable urban places with human scale: close tables, a working counter, stools or banquettes, tiled surfaces, warm timber, glass, plaster, worn metal and selective greenery. The collected results include interiors, sidewalk seating, small courtyards and street-facing entrances. Match the requested interior or exterior explicitly.

**Light and palette:** Daylight through a front window or doorway, warm pendant practicals where visible, green or blue tile, red or yellow accents and credible skin tones. For an evening brief, retain local pools of light and deeper shadows rather than washing the entire room orange.

**Composition:** Use the counter, doorway or table spacing to orient the scene. Leave staff and customers room to move. People can be chatting, reading menus, serving or clearing a table; their small actions should motivate the atmosphere. Facades and signs are useful arrival references, not substitutes for an interior.

**Inspected candidates:** L04-008, L04-012, L04-017, L04-024, L04-025, L04-034, L04-076, L04-079 and L04-098. For outdoor seating, inspect L04-009, L04-031, L04-042 or L04-062.

## L05 — Urban studios and offices

**Character:** Working spaces with character rather than generic corporate staging. Exposed ceilings, concrete or brick, tall windows and steel-framed partitions; timber desks, shelving, books, pinned work, practical task lamps and modest greenery. The collection ranges from individual desks to shared studios, meeting areas and softer breakout seating.

**Light and palette:** Broad side daylight across desks, warm wood and restrained olive, blue or rust against neutral structure. Keep monitors, paper, metal and fabric visually distinct. Brighter art and eclectic furnishing can identify a creative studio without turning the entire office into an art installation.

**Composition:** Show a believable work surface and usable circulation. Let a monitor, sketch, notebook or sample indicate the job without cluttering every surface. For people, use focused work, a short exchange or a natural pause. Keep the role and casting requested in the brief.

**Inspected candidates:** L05-001, L05-005, L05-008, L05-009, L05-020, L05-024, L05-025, L05-038, L05-040 and L05-090. Treat outdoor approaches, collages and title-heavy cards as separate review cases.

## L06 — Warm, human-scale portrait settings

**Character:** The seed is an environmental portrait in a warm domestic sitting area, not an empty architecture image. A rust-colored wall, timber shelving, pale ceramics, books, woven upholstery and a relaxed seated person form the direction. Use this collection for the relationship between a person and their room: reading corners, home workspaces, quiet lounges and intimate cafe settings.

**Light and palette:** Soft but directional window light, natural falloff and warm brown, terracotta, cream and olive. The shelves and foreground can be softly out of focus while remaining recognizable. Keep believable skin and material texture.

**Composition:** Human viewing distance, an unforced posture and a small action such as reading, listening or pausing at a desk. The seed's doorway or foreground frame creates depth. Related portraits that show almost no environment are performance or light references, not sufficient location references on their own. Do not copy a real person's identity or infer a profession from the image.

**Inspected candidates:** L06-001 (user seed), L06-002, L06-006, L06-014, L06-020, L06-032, L06-037, L06-041, L06-047, L06-063, L06-089 and L06-096.

## L07 — Small patios and planted courtyards

**Character:** A compact outdoor room enclosed by neighboring walls. The seed combines brick and pale plaster, a dark door, a narrow floor runner, terracotta pots, climbing foliage, small seating and simple strings of bulbs. Keep the small, personal scale and the relationship between the patio and its adjoining room.

**Light and palette:** Soft sky light and directional sun where the enclosure permits it. Warm brick, terracotta, cream, dark green and the natural variety of leaves and flowers. At dusk, existing bulbs and lanterns add warm points while the sky and distant surfaces stay cooler.

**Composition:** Use the enclosing walls, doorway and plants to layer depth. Leave a credible walking path and enough space to sit; avoid filling every inch with props. The location can host a quiet coffee, a small meal, reading or plant care. Distinguish a courtyard, balcony and rooftop when the brief specifies one.

**Seed anchor:** L07-001, subject to current review state.

## Review interpretation

The user keeps every saved image in this collection unless explicitly rejected. Agent flags suggest closer inspection, not exclusion. Pinterest recommendations can drift across location types, include title graphics, product imagery or render-like spaces, and repeat an image across different sources. Review flags identify obvious setting or graphic mismatches without recording a user rejection. Select and inspect one on-theme, non-rejected location image to pair with the character reference. Copy its spatial and photographic construction, then specify five concrete element changes that fit the story; don't average the collection into one style. All user rejections apply by image identifier across the seven sets.
